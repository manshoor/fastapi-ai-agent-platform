# House rules

Quiet hours start at 22:00 and end at 07:00 every day. Please keep music and power tools quiet during quiet hours.

Pets are welcome. Dogs must be kept on a lead in the lobby, the lifts and the garden.

Bicycles are stored in the basement bike room. Do not leave bicycles in the stairwells.

Garbage and recycling go to the bin room next to the garage entrance. Glass is collected on Mondays.

Smoking is not allowed on balconies, in the stairwells or anywhere inside the building.
