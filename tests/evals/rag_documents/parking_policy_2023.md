# Maple Court parking policy 2023 (superseded)

This policy was replaced on 1 March 2025 and no longer applies. It is kept for reference only.

## Resident permits (2023)

Under the 2023 policy, each apartment could hold two resident parking permits. A resident permit cost €38 per month and had to be renewed every year in January.

## Visitor parking (2023)

Visitors paid €3 per hour for visitor parking. A visitor could stay for up to 5 hours. Overnight visitor parking was allowed on Fridays and Saturdays.

## Moving vans (2023)

A moving van could park in the loading bay for the whole day without a permit.
