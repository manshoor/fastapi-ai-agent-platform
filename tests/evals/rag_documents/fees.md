# Maple Court fees and payments

This page lists the charges that Maple Court residents pay to the management company, how to pay them and what happens when a payment is late. All amounts include VAT.

## Service charge

The monthly service charge covers cleaning of the shared areas, the caretaker, lift maintenance, the garden and the building insurance. It is due on the 1st day of each month and is paid by direct debit.

## Late payment

If the service charge has not been received by the 10th day of the month, a reminder is sent by email. A late payment fee of €15 is added to the account for each month that a payment is late. After two missed payments, the management company may start a formal collection process.

## Keys and access cards

### Key replacement

A replacement apartment key costs €25. Order it from the management office; it is ready within 3 working days. Keys for the bike room and the mailbox are not replaced individually: the lock is changed and two new keys are issued.

The cost of a new lock and two keys is €60 for the bike room and €45 for the mailbox. Collect them from the caretaker's office, which is open on weekdays from 09:00 to 12:00. Bring your resident card when you collect anything.

### Access card

An extra access card for the front door and the garage costs €20. Each apartment may hold at most four access cards. A lost card is blocked as soon as you report it, and the replacement costs the same as an extra card.

## Bookings

### Guest room fee

The guest room costs €35 per night. The fee is paid in the resident app when you book.

### Party room fee

The party room costs €50 per booking plus a €100 deposit. The deposit is returned within 14 days after the room has been checked by the caretaker.

## Refunds

Refunds of deposits and cancelled bookings are paid to the account that paid the original amount. Cash is not accepted for any charge.
