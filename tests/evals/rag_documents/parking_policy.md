# Maple Court parking policy

This is the current parking policy for Maple Court. It took effect on 1 March 2025.

## Resident permits

Each apartment may hold one resident parking permit for the garage. A resident permit costs €45 per month and is renewed automatically. Apply for a permit through the resident app; the permit is a sticker for the inside of the windscreen.

Electric vehicles may charge only at the four marked charging bays. Charging costs €0.35 per kWh and is billed with the service charge.

## Visitor parking

Visitor parking costs €2 per hour, paid at the machine by the garage entrance or in the parking app. A visitor may park for at most 3 hours in a row in the visitor bays. Visitor bays may not be used overnight.

## Moving vans and contractors

A moving van may park in the loading bay for up to 4 hours with a van permit. A van permit costs €15 per day and must be requested at least 2 working days before the move.

## Towing

Vehicles parked in a fire lane or in front of the garage door are towed at the owner's cost without warning.
