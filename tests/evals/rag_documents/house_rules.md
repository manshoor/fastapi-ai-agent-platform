# Maple Court house rules

These rules apply to every resident, guest and contractor at Maple Court. The management office publishes them, reviews them every January and posts changes on the lobby notice board.

## Noise

### Quiet hours

Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 07:00 on weekdays. On Saturdays and Sundays they run from 23:00 to 09:00. During quiet hours, keep music, television and conversation at a level that cannot be heard in the corridor or in the next apartment.

Parties in apartments need a notice on the lobby board at least 3 days in advance, and they must still end by the start of quiet hours.

### Renovation work

Loud renovation work is allowed only between 08:00 and 18:00 on weekdays. Loud work includes drilling, hammering, sanding floors, cutting tiles and removing walls or fitted furniture.

Before the work starts, agree a schedule with your contractor and keep a copy of it in the apartment. The contractor must carry the schedule and show it to the caretaker on request. Contractors park in the visitor bays like any other visitor, and they may not leave tools, materials or waste in the corridors, the stairwells or the bin room overnight.

Protect the floor of the lift with the blankets the caretaker provides, and use the service lift for heavy materials. Contractors may not use the main lifts for materials at any time. Dust sheets must cover the corridor carpet outside your door while work is going on.

Remove all building waste yourself or ask the contractor to take it away. Building waste never goes in the household bins or in the bulky waste collection, and the cost of clearing waste left behind is charged to the apartment.

Residents must notify management at least 5 working days before loud work starts. Quiet work such as painting or putting up shelves with a hand screwdriver needs no notice. Loud renovation work is not allowed on public holidays.

## Pets

### Dogs

Each apartment may keep up to two dogs. A dog that weighs more than 25 kg needs written approval from management before it moves in. Dogs must be on a lead in the lobby, the lifts and the courtyard garden.

### Cats and small animals

Cats may be kept indoors only and must not be let out onto the corridors. Small animals such as rabbits, guinea pigs and birds are allowed without approval. Reptiles need written approval from management.

### Pet registration

Register every pet with the management office within 14 days of moving in or of the pet arriving. The registration form asks for the species, the name and a photo. There is no registration fee for assistance animals.

## Balconies

### Barbecues

Charcoal and gas barbecues are not allowed on balconies. Electric grills are allowed if the balcony has an outdoor socket.

### Laundry and plants

Laundry may be dried on balconies only below the height of the railing. Plant pots must stand on the inside of the railing, never on top of it or hanging over the edge.

## Shared spaces

### Roof terrace

The roof terrace is open every day from 07:00 to 22:00, from 1 May to 30 September. It is closed during storms and when the caretaker posts a closure notice at the stairwell door.

Access is through the stairwell door on the sixth floor, because the lifts stop one level below. The door opens with your access card and closes by itself; never prop it open, as it is also a fire door.

The caretaker cleans the space on Monday and Thursday mornings and waters the planters in the evening during dry weeks. Report broken furniture or loose tiles to the caretaker the same day.

The furniture, the sunshades and the planters belong to the building. Do not move the planters, because they hold the weight down in strong wind. Close the sunshades when you leave, and put the cushions back in the storage chest by the stairs.

Glass bottles and glasses are not allowed up there; use plastic cups. Music is allowed only through headphones or at a volume that cannot be heard from the apartments below. Smoking is not allowed anywhere on the building's roof.

Children under 12 must be with an adult at all times. Ball games, skateboards and scooters are not allowed, and nothing may be thrown over the parapet.

Each apartment may bring up to four guests at a time. The resident who invites the guests must stay with them for the whole visit and is responsible for any damage. Reservations are not possible; the space is shared on a first come, first served basis.

### Guest room

The guest room on the ground floor can be booked for visitors who stay overnight. It has a double bed, a sofa bed, a small kitchen corner and its own shower room.

It is meant for family and friends of residents who live too far away to go home the same day. It is not a hotel, and the management office does not provide breakfast, cleaning during a stay or a reception service.

Smoking and candles are not allowed inside, and the window must be closed when nobody is in the room. Noise rules apply to visitors in the same way as to residents.

Book it through the resident app at least 7 days ahead. A booking is confirmed only after the payment has been received. Cancellations made more than 48 hours before arrival are refunded in full; later cancellations are not refunded.

Collect the key from the caretaker on the day of arrival between 14:00 and 18:00, and return it by 11:00 on the day of departure. Visitors must not have copies made of the key. A lost key is replaced at the resident's cost.

Bed linen and towels are provided and changed between bookings. Leave the kitchen corner clean and take your rubbish to the bin room before you leave. Pets are not allowed in it, and it may not be sublet or used for paid stays.

Up to two visitors may stay at the same time, for at most five nights per booking. Each apartment may book it for at most ten nights per calendar year.

### Party room

The party room next to the courtyard garden can be booked for private events of up to 30 people. It has tables, chairs, a fridge, a dishwasher and a small sound system.

Book it through the resident app at least 14 days ahead. Only one event can be booked per day, and each apartment may book it at most six times per calendar year. The resident who books must be present for the whole event.

Decorations may be attached only with the removable hooks in the cupboard; tape, nails and pins are not allowed on the walls. Confetti, smoke machines and fireworks are not allowed. Food may be brought in or delivered, but cooking on open flames is not allowed.

Guests arrive and leave through the courtyard door, not through the lobby. Keep the courtyard door closed during the event, so that the music does not reach the apartments above.

The booking fee and the deposit are paid in the resident app. The caretaker checks the room the next morning and reports any damage or missing items, which are deducted from the deposit. Lost glasses and plates are charged at their replacement price.

Events must end by 23:00. The room must be cleaned, the dishwasher emptied and the key returned to the caretaker by 10:00 the next morning.

## Waste

Household waste and recycling go to the bin room next to the garage entrance. Glass is collected on Mondays. Bulky waste is collected on the first Saturday of each month; put it in the marked area of the garage by 08:00.

## Bicycles

Bicycles are stored in the basement bike room. Each apartment has two numbered places. Bicycles left in the stairwells or the lobby are removed after a written warning.
